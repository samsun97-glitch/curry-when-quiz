How to Make Curry

중1 영어 · 접속사 when

카레 레시피 읽기

Do you know how to make curry? I’ll show you my simple recipe. First, cut onions, beef, potatoes, and carrots in cubes.

① When is the pot hot, add the onions and fry them. ② When the onions are ready, add the meat and fry it. Add potatoes and carrots ③ when is the meat ready and fry them.

④ When they are ready, put water in the pot. ⑤ When boils the water, add curry powder. Boil for 5 more minutes. You can have a delicious meal ⑥ when the curry is done!

정답 ① · ③ · ⑤

  • ① When the pot is hot
  • ③ when the meat is ready
  • ⑤ When the water boils
